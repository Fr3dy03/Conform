# Brand and asset usage

Conform is an independent BOT Chain testnet tool. It is not an official BOT
Chain product and must not imply endorsement.

## BOT Chain logo

The official logo is stored unmodified at
`apps/web/public/brand/bot-chain-logo.svg` and is used in the shared footer on
every page.

Rules:

- Use the supplied SVG without recoloring, cropping, redrawing, rotating or
  changing its proportions.
- Preserve the logo's clear space. Do not place text or controls against the
  mark.
- Keep the logo secondary to Conform's own wordmark.
- Link the logo to <https://www.botchain.ai/en/>.
- Do not use the logo as a Conform product icon or social avatar.

Source and brand guidance:
[BOT Chain Brand Kit](https://www.botchain.ai/static/BOT%20Chain%20Assets/BOT%20Chain%20Brand%20Kit.zip).

## Typography

- UI text uses a system sans-serif fallback stack.
- Hashes, addresses, method names, counts and labels that need alignment use a
  monospace stack.
- No remotely hosted font is required to render the app.
- Data text remains legible at compact sizes; exported values are also
  available in the JSON report.

## Voice

- State what was observed, not what is assumed.
- Distinguish submitted, pending, confirmed and failed.
- Say **unknown** when sponsorship has not been proven.
- Keep demo evidence visibly labelled.
- Prefer actionable failure guidance over alarmist copy.
- Do not claim full ERC-4337 compliance from a sampled method matrix.
